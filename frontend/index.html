Stock Prices

A cycle starts at a high price, falls to a low, and ends on the first day the price is back at that high. Each colored bracket is one cycle. Its top line marks the high price, its lowest point marks the low, and its color shows how far the price fell. Smaller cycles often sit inside larger ones. Falls that haven't recovered yet aren't included. Falls below the lowest threshold count toward the cycle total but aren't drawn.

Stock cycle period

Each box shows the number of weeks from a cycle's high back to the day the price recovered to it, grouped by how far the price fell. n is the number of cycles in each group.
